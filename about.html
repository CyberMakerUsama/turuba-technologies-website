About TURUBA Technologies

Last updated: September 24, 2026

TURUBA Technologies is a technology company focused on building practical digital products with an emphasis on usability, privacy, security, and accessible technology.

Security Vault

Security Vault is our password and secure-file management product. The current product includes password management, password generation, secure encrypted-file storage, security-code storage, email verification, two-factor authentication, biometric unlock, vault auto-lock, active-session management, security activity and alerts, and account/password recovery controls.

Our approach

We develop products iteratively, test them across supported flows, improve security over time, and communicate product capabilities without making absolute security guarantees.
