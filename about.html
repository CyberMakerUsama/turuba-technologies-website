About TURUBA Technologies

Last updated: September 17, 2026

TURUBA Technologies is a technology company focused on building practical digital products with an emphasis on usability, privacy, security, and accessible technology.

Our Product

Security Vault is our password and secure-file management product. It is designed to help users manage credentials, use additional authentication controls, review security activity, generate passwords, and store encrypted files.

Our Approach

We aim to develop products iteratively, improve security over time, and communicate product capabilities accurately rather than making absolute security guarantees.
