Security Vault

Security Vault is our password and secure-file management product. The current product includes password management, password generation, secure encrypted-file storage, security-code storage, email verification, two-factor authentication, biometric unlock, vault auto-lock, active-session management, security activity and alerts, and account/password recovery controls.
