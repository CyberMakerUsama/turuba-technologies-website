What is Money Ledger?

Money Ledger is a personal record book for money you have lent or borrowed. It helps you remember who the entry is for, the amount, any due date, the payments recorded and what remains.

What can you track?

  • Money lent: an amount someone owes you.
  • Money borrowed: an amount you owe someone else.
  • Repayments: payments you record against an entry and the remaining balance.
  • Due dates and notes: reminders about when a balance is expected and what it relates to.

Amounts in the current Money Ledger are recorded in Indian rupees (INR). It is a record-keeping tool: it does not transfer money, connect to a bank, collect a payment or verify that a payment took place.

How to use it

  1. Unlock your vault and open Money Ledger on Android or the web.
  2. Add a person, choose whether you lent or borrowed the money, and enter the amount.
  3. Add an optional due date or note.
  4. Record each repayment as it happens and check the remaining balance.

How is the record protected?

Money entry content is encrypted on Security Vault's server. The server holds the key and decrypts entries after authorized access, so the ledger is not currently end-to-end encrypted. Read the Security page for more detail.
